## Recording preparation

- Use a separate backed-up demo save; preserve the player's normal collection. Have 20 Sparkles for fusion, a Life Seed for the photo creature and a Craft Gem if saving a photo item.
- Record model waits in full first. The final three-minute edit can shorten waits with visible captions; keep raw footage as evidence. Fill every [recorded time] with an actual measured value, or use “wait shortened” without a fabricated number.
- If a provider is busy, show the retry/refund honestly or record another genuine successful take. A local traced sprite or photo fallback must be labeled as local, not Muse Image output.
- Preserve the timer caption, original photo comparison, parent comparison, ability effect, estimated-size wording and actual bloom. Banter and quests are optional supporting footage if there is room; do not displace the core sequence to squeeze them in.
- This script does not establish physical-camera reliability, a fresh complete successful Meta run, stable 60 FPS or production deployment readiness. The latest development scene was around 30 FPS after rendering improvements.
- Review asset attribution; show work developed during the event accurately. Keep keys, private photo backgrounds, billing screens and unrelated account information out of the capture.
